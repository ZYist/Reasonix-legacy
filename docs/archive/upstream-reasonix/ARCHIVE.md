# Upstream Reasonix documentation archive

> **Read-only historical snapshot — 2026-07-15**
>
> This directory preserves documentation inherited from the historical upstream
> project, [`esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix`](https://github.com/esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix).
> It is retained for provenance and design research only. It is **not** the
> operating guide for the maintained `reasonix-legacy` `dev` branch.

## Safety boundary

The archived pages may describe product surfaces and delivery paths that no
longer exist in this fork, including the Web dashboard, Tauri desktop app,
upstream releases, upstream package names, and obsolete installation or setup
links. Links and commands inside the snapshot may be stale. Do not treat them as
supported behavior and do not submit credentials to endpoints referenced only by
archived content.

Use the [current documentation hub](../../README.md) for installation, CLI,
configuration, architecture, and channel instructions.

## Preservation policy

- Snapshot date: **2026-07-15**.
- Content below `root/` and `site/` is preserved as inherited history; broken
  links and obsolete claims are intentionally not rewritten.
- Current governance, security, contributor, benchmark, package-local, channel,
  lifecycle-testing, and planning documents remain outside this archive.
- Historical upstream URLs may remain inside the snapshot for attribution and
  traceability. Current maintenance links must point to
  `ZYist/reasonix-legacy`.
